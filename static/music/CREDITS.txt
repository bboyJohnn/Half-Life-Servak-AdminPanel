Background music for the HL1 server web panel
=============================================

All tracks below are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain
dedication); the license was checked on each track's own OpenGameArt page.
No attribution is required; credits are listed as a courtesy.
Files were converted to MP3 (VBR -q:a 4, 44.1 kHz stereo) and their volume
was matched to about -16 LUFS so the playlist plays at an even level.
